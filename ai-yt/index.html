AI YT Automation

AI YT Automation is a creator-operated, private production workflow under development for original English-language animated mystery YouTube Shorts. This information site explains the app's purpose and data use; it is not a public sign-up service.

What it does

  • Reads the operator's Google Sheets story queue, approval and publishing metadata.
  • Retrieves approved video MP4 assets and QA reports from the operator's Google Drive.
  • Connects to the operator's authorized YouTube channel to submit video uploads and check their results.
  • Runs an operator-controlled n8n workflow on private AWS infrastructure.

Only Google accounts explicitly authorized by the operator may connect. Uploads and publishing depend on operator-set permissions and review gates.
