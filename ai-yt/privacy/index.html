Privacy Policy

Effective November 16, 2026.

AI YT Automation is a private, creator-operated video workflow under development. It does not offer public accounts.

Google data accessed

  • Sheets: story IDs, content approval, video queues, titles, descriptions, scheduling and upload records.
  • Drive: selected MP4 files, file IDs, file metadata and QA reports.
  • YouTube: authorized channel information, uploads, selected privacy status, returned video IDs and upload status.

Purposes

Information is used to organize and check approved content, retrieve selected videos, perform owner-authorized YouTube uploads and record their results. This tool is not used to sell Google user data or target advertising.

Storage and sharing

The operator hosts n8n, credentials and workflow records on AWS. Video files may be stored in Google Drive, and approved uploads are sent to YouTube according to the operator's configured visibility. Google and infrastructure providers process information under their applicable policies. No Google account credentials are exposed on this public website.

Retention and user controls

Operational data and tokens are retained for the workflow until the operator removes them or they are no longer needed. The operator may delete n8n credentials and revoke consent in Google Account permissions. Revoking access will not automatically delete previously uploaded videos or archived files.

Security, updates and contact

The application restricts dashboard access through a private network. Actual retention and permissions depend on its operator-managed configuration. Material changes to data use will be reflected on this page. Contact rebuildai9@gmail.com for questions or deletion requests.
